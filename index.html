Patrick Williams – Estate Planner

About Patrick

I started Patrick D Williams Estate Planners because I saw too many families left with uncertainty and unnecessary stress after a loved one passed away. I wanted to make estate planning straightforward, affordable, and human.

With over 20 years of experience in accountancy and financial planning, I understand the importance of getting the details right. But more than that, I know that talking about Wills and Powers of Attorney isn't easy. I take time to listen, explain everything in plain English, and make sure you feel confident about the decisions you make.

I cover London, Birmingham, Nottingham, Manchester, Bristol, and Leeds – and I offer home visits if that's easier for you.

Our Services

Straightforward services with fixed fees – no surprises.

Will Writing

A professionally drafted Will that clearly sets out your wishes. Includes advice on guardians for children and inheritance tax planning.

£295 + VAT (single)
£495 + VAT (mirror Wills for couples)

Lasting Power of Attorney

Health & welfare and property & financial affairs LPAs. We handle the registration with the Office of the Public Guardian.

£395 + VAT (single LPA)
£695 + VAT (both LPAs)

Trusts & Asset Protection

Trusts to protect your home, savings and investments from care fees and inheritance tax. Bespoke advice tailored to your situation.

From £795 + VAT (depending on complexity)

Executor Service

We can act as professional executor of your Will, taking the burden off your family during a difficult time. Includes full estate administration.

From £1,200 + VAT (depending on estate size)
