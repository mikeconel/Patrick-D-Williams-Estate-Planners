Lasting Powers of Attorney

An LPA is your safeguard against the unexpected — a legal framework that ensures your finances, property, and personal welfare remain in trusted hands if you can no longer manage them yourself.

Two Instruments, Complete Protection

LPA Type One - Property & Financial Affairs

Empowers your chosen attorneys to manage your bank accounts, pay bills, collect benefits, sell property, and make investment decisions on your behalf.

  • Can be used while you still have capacity (with your consent)
  • Covers all financial decisions including tax affairs
  • Can include specific restrictions and conditions

LPA Type Two - Health & Welfare

Grants your attorneys authority over medical treatment, care arrangements, daily routines, and — if you choose — life-sustaining treatment decisions.

  • Only activated when you lack mental capacity
  • Covers medical treatment and care decisions
  • Can include life-sustaining treatment preferences

Why Act Now

01

Maintain Control While You Can

An LPA is drafted while you have full mental capacity, ensuring the people you trust are legally empowered to step in if you become unable to make decisions — whether through illness, injury, or age.

02

Avoid Court of Protection Applications

Without an LPA, your family must apply to the Court of Protection to manage your affairs — a costly, time-consuming, and emotionally draining process that can take months and offers no guarantee of outcome.

03

Cover Both Health and Finances

Two distinct instruments — one for Property & Financial Affairs, one for Health & Welfare — ensure comprehensive coverage. Each can be tailored with specific instructions and restrictions.

04

Built-In Safeguards

Your LPA includes named persons to be notified, restrictions on attorney powers, and preferences that guide your attorneys in making decisions that align with your values and wishes.

Our Process

I

Consultation

We discuss your circumstances and help you choose the right attorneys and replacement attorneys.

II

Drafting

We prepare both LPA documents with your specific preferences, restrictions, and named persons.

III

Certification

A certificate provider confirms you understand the LPA and are not under undue pressure.

IV

Registration
