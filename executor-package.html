The Complete Brief

Executor Package

Administering an estate can be burdensome and confusing. Our Executor Package provides your appointed executor with everything they need to navigate probate efficiently — reducing delay, anxiety, and professional costs.

Why an Executor Package Matters

Acting as an executor is a significant responsibility — and one most people undertake without any prior experience. The average probate process takes 9–12 months, and executors who are unprepared face unnecessary delays, mounting costs, and the risk of personal liability for errors.

Our Executor Package is designed to be handed to your executor on your death — a complete operational manual that transforms a complex legal process into a structured, manageable workflow. Your executor will know exactly what to do, who to contact, and how to do it.

By preparing this package as part of your estate planning, you reduce the emotional and practical burden on the person you have chosen to administer your estate — allowing them to focus on the task rather than learning it from scratch.

The Executor's Burden Without Preparation:

  • Locating the original Will and understanding its terms
  • Identifying every asset and liability across multiple institutions
  • Navigating HMRC forms and inheritance tax calculations
  • Applying for the Grant of Probate without legal training
  • Managing family expectations and beneficiary communications
  • Producing estate accounts for final distribution

What's Inside the Package

Part I - The Executor's Brief

Your role, your duties, and your legal obligations explained in plain English. What the Grant of Probate is and why you need it.

Part II - Asset & Liability Ledger

Every asset and liability of the estate catalogued, valued, and cross-referenced to the relevant institution with account numbers.

Part III - The Probate Timeline

A chronological roadmap from the date of death to the final distribution — with estimated durations for each stage.

Part IV - Correspondence Templates

Pre-drafted letters addressing banks, insurers, HMRC, DWP, pension providers, and utility companies — ready to print and send.

Part V - IHT Computation

The inheritance tax calculation with reference to all available reliefs, exemptions, and nil-rate bands relevant to the estate.

Part VI - Beneficiary Communication

Guidance on keeping beneficiaries informed — including template updates at key milestones in the administration process.

Part VII - Final Accounts & Distribution

A framework for preparing the estate accounts and effecting the final distribution in accordance with the Will.

The Benefits

01

Complete Asset & Liability Register

A comprehensive schedule of all known assets — property, bank accounts, investments, pensions, life policies, and personal possessions — together with an inventory of liabilities. The executor knows exactly what the estate comprises from day one.

02

Institution & Point-of-Contact Directory

Every bank, building society, investment platform, utility provider, insurer, and professional adviser listed with account references and contact details — eliminating the executor's need to search through decades of paperwork.

03

Step-by-Step Probate Roadmap

A plain-English timeline of the probate process: registering the death, valuing the estate, completing the IHT return, applying for the Grant of Probate, collecting in assets, settling liabilities, and distributing to beneficiaries.

04

Prepared Documentation Suite

Template letters for notifying financial institutions, utility companies, HMRC, and the Department for Work and Pensions — together with the PA1P probate application form pre-populated with the estate's known particulars.

05

Inheritance Tax Guidance

A clear explanation of the IHT thresholds, reliefs, and exemptions relevant to your estate — including the residence nil-rate band, charitable exemptions, and business property relief — with guidance on completing form IHT205 or IHT400.
